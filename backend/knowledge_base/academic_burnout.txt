# Mental Wellness Psychoeducation: Overcoming Academic Burnout

Title: Recognizing and Recovering from Academic Burnout
Category: Academic Burnout
Audience: Higher Education Students

## Identifying Burnout vs. Normal Fatigue
Burnout is characterized by emotional exhaustion, depersonalization/cynicism towards academic goals, and feelings of reduced personal accomplishment. Unlike normal tiredness which resolves after a good night's rest, burnout lingers and diminishes enthusiasm for subjects you previously enjoyed.

## Core Recovery Steps:
1. Active Rest vs. Passive Numbing:
Scrolling through social media or binge-watching video streams feels effortless but doesn't restore cognitive energy. Engage in active rest: walks in nature, light creative hobbies, or gentle stretching.

2. Re-evaluating the Perfectionism Trap:
Perfectionism often masks fear of failure. Shift your standard from unattainable flawless performance to "sustainable excellence". Good enough work submitted on time is infinitely better than overdue paralysis.

3. Disconnecting to Reconnect:
Designate at least one half-day or evening per week completely free of coursework, lecture recordings, and campus emails.

4. Seeking Guidance Early:
Talk with academic advisors about spreading out difficult seminar loads, and consult campus counselors to build resilience strategies.
