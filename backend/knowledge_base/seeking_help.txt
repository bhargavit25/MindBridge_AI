# Mental Wellness Psychoeducation: Seeking Professional Mental Healthcare

Title: Navigating Mental Health Care and Stigma Reduction
Category: Seeking Professional Help
Audience: Higher Education Students

## Overcoming Mental Health Stigma
Seeking counseling is a courageous sign of self-awareness and personal responsibility, not a weakness. Just as an athlete consults a physical therapist for a muscular sprain, a student benefits from consulting a psychological counselor to navigate cognitive distortion, emotional turbulence, and life transitions.

## Types of Support Available:
1. Licensed Counselors & Therapists:
Provide structured modalities such as Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT), and Solution-Focused Brief Therapy.

2. Campus Student Wellness Centers:
Offer confidential, free or subsidized consultations, support groups, and crisis intervention.

3. Psychiatrists:
Medical doctors specializing in mental health who can evaluate biological components and discuss medical treatments when clinically indicated.

## What to Expect in Your First Session:
Your counselor will discuss confidentiality boundaries, ask what brought you to therapy, listen without judgment, and collaboratively outline goals for your well-being. You are always in control of what you choose to share.
