# Mental Wellness Psychoeducation: Mindfulness & Breathing Exercises

Title: Mindful Breathing and Emotional Regulation Techniques
Category: Mindfulness & Breathing
Audience: University Students

## What is Mindfulness?
Mindfulness is the non-judgmental awareness of present-moment experience, including thoughts, emotions, and bodily sensations. Rather than fighting unpleasant sensations or ruminating over past exams, mindfulness fosters a compassionate stance of observation.

## Practical Exercises for Busy Students:
1. The 4-7-8 Relaxing Breath Technique:
- Exhale completely through your mouth with a whoosh sound.
- Close mouth and inhale quietly through your nose for 4 counts.
- Hold breath for 7 counts.
- Exhale completely through mouth with a whoosh sound for 8 counts.
- Repeat for 4 full breath cycles.

2. Three-Minute Breathing Space:
- Minute 1: Awareness. Ask "What is my experience right now? What thoughts, feelings, and physical sensations are present?"
- Minute 2: Gathering. Focus your full attention on the physical movement of the breath entering and leaving your abdomen.
- Minute 3: Expanding. Expand awareness from your breath to encompass your entire body posture and facial expression.

3. Body Scan Before Study:
Spend two minutes scanning from top of your head down to your toes. Notice where you carry tension (jaw, temples, neck, shoulders) and exhale deeply while consciously softening those areas.
